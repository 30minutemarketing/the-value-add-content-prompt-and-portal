Start With The Topic

This portal is designed to stop generic AI content before it starts. You are going to feed the AI your topic, your proof, your observations, and the content format you want.

What Does Everyone Already Know?

Summarize the existing information, common advice, repeated talking points, or basic explanations people can already find elsewhere.

What Do You Know From Experience?

Add the human layer. Include personal experience, observations, lessons learned, mistakes made, wins, failures, or real-world results.

Enter Any Data That Proves Or Supports Your Point

Add any type of data you have. This can be your own experience data, content analytics, sales data, customer feedback, survey results, test results, research data, industry statistics, case study results, or examples from credible sources.

What Research Or Outside Proof Supports The Point?

Add research findings, statistics, studies, surveys, case studies, or credible examples. If you do not have research yet, explain what kind of research you want the AI to suggest or help you look for.

What Are You Adding That Is New?

This is the contribution. Add your original insights, opinions, conclusions, patterns, predictions, or practical meaning based on the information.

Choose The Content Output

Tell the AI exactly what to create from your proof. You can generate a social post, article, video script, email, lesson, or case study.

Generate Your AI LLM Meta Prompt

Click the button below to create a complete meta prompt. Paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Grok. You can also copy, download, or reset the portal.

Final check: If someone read this, would they learn something they could not have learned from ChatGPT alone? If the answer is no, keep improving it until the answer is yes.
