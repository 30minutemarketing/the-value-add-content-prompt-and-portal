Start With The Big Idea

This portal is designed to stop generic AI content before it starts. You are going to feed the AI your topic, your proof, your observations, and the content format you want.

What Everyone Already Says

Capture the generic advice, common belief, or surface-level talking point. This gives the AI something to move beyond.

Add Your Experience Layer

This is where your content becomes hard to copy. Add what you have personally seen, tested, learned, or noticed.

Enter Your Proof Data

Use your own numbers. These can come from YouTube, email, affiliate dashboards, sales pages, opt-in pages, Skool, analytics, surveys, or customer feedback.

Add Outside Research Or Proof

This step is optional but powerful. Add credible research, market data, case studies, platform data, surveys, or outside examples. If you do not have outside research yet, tell the AI to suggest what kind of research would strengthen the piece.

Define The Value-Add Insight

This is the contribution. Not just what the data says, but what it means.

Choose The Content Output

Tell the AI exactly what to create from your proof. You can generate a social post, article, video script, email, lesson, or case study.
